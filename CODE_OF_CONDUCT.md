# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for everyone contributing to or interacting with this project.

All contributors and participants are expected to treat others with respect, regardless of background, experience, identity, or level of technical knowledge.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Using respectful and professional language
* Being open to constructive feedback and differing viewpoints
* Providing constructive criticism
* Collaborating in good faith
* Respecting the contributions and work of others
* Maintaining a professional environment when communicating about the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
* Insulting, threatening, or derogatory comments
* Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate language
* Publishing or sharing another person's private information without permission
* Deliberately disrupting or interfering with the work of other contributors
* Other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic environment

## Responsibilities

Project contributors and maintainers are expected to uphold these standards when working on, discussing, or contributing to the project.

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other project content that does not comply with these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within the project repository and other spaces directly associated with the project, including project discussions, documentation, and communication between contributors.

It also applies when individuals are representing the project in public or academic settings.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please raise the matter with the project maintainer or supervisor through the appropriate project communication channel.

Reports should be made in good faith and will be treated respectfully and, where appropriate, confidentially.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on principles commonly used in open-source and academic projects and has been adapted for this project.
